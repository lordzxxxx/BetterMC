3. SUMMARY OF LIVESTREAMED BAC PROCEEDINGS & POLICY DISCUSSIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3.1 Institutional Mandate for Livestreamed Bidding [CONFIRMED & REPORTED]:
    - Under an administrative directive issued by City Mayor Jay Warren R. Pabillaran
      in mid-2022 and maintained through 2025–2026, all Bids and Awards Committee (BAC)
      proceedings—including Pre-Procurement Conferences, Pre-Bid Conferences, Eligibility
      Checks, Opening of Bids, and Post-Qualification announcements—are mandated to be
      broadcast live on Facebook via the BAC LGU Malaybalay page (facebook.com/bacmalaybalay)
      and the Official City Government of Malaybalay page (profile.php?id=100063747444247),
      and cataloged under the City website's 'FB LIVE of Public Bidding Archive'.
    - Participating Agencies & Offices in BAC Proceedings:
      * Bids and Awards Committee (BAC), BAC Secretariat, and Technical Working Group (TWG)
      * City Engineering Office (CEO) — civil works, road concreting, multi-purpose
        buildings, water systems, school buildings, and drainage projects
      * City General Services Office (CGSO) — equipment, vehicles, and supply consolidation
      * City Health Office (CHO) & CDRRMO — medicines, laboratory supplies, rescue vehicles
      * City Mayor's Office (CMO) & CEEDMO-MCGWSS — heavy equipment and barangay support
      * DepEd Schools Division of Malaybalay City & 46 Barangay Councils (end-user units)

3.2 Reported Fiscal Impact Discussed by City Leadership [REPORTED DISCUSSION]:
    - According to statements by Mayor Jay Warren R. Pabillaran reported by the
      Philippine Daily Inquirer and Coverstory.ph, livestreaming BAC bidding sessions
      increased bidder participation and competitive pricing, generating reported
      cumulative procurement savings of PHP 361,870,000.00 from mid-2022 through 2025:
      * Mid-2022 through 2024 reported savings: PHP 241,920,000.00
      * Full-year 2025 reported savings: PHP 119,950,000.00
    - City officials stated that funds saved from competitive bidding were reallocated
      through supplemental appropriations to support:
      * 24/7 antivenom serum availability and mobile health clinics
      * Resettlement and housing support for disaster-affected families
      * Annual educational scholarships for more than 400 students
      * Year-round hiring of professional firefighters and emergency responders
    - Verification Note: BetterMC distinguishes this reported procurement-savings
      statement (difference between Approved Budget for the Contract [ABC] and winning
      bid prices) from accounting 'net operating balance' or audited cash on hand.
