## 3. Source Conflicts & Corrections Documented Transparently

- **DILG CLGOO Email Conflict:** `sakata.malaybalaycity.gov.ph/departments-offices/` mistakenly duplicates CCRO's `malaybalayccro@gmail.com` under DILG, while `/contact-us/` prints `dilg.buk.malaybalay@gmail.cm` (typo `.cm`). Both are flagged on `dilg.bettermalaybalay.cloud` / `/offices/dilg` with phone confirmation advice (`0926-880-6529`).
- **CHO Mobile Variants:** Charter p. 729 (`0965-094-9150 / 0917-719-4588`) vs `/contact-us/` (`0965-094-9105 / 0966-475-6979 / (088) 813-2877`). All verified numbers are surfaced.
- **PESO Phone Variants:** Charter pp. 550+ (`0907-688-5735`) vs `/contact-us/` (`0915-542-3434 / 0912-163-5505`). All three verified numbers are surfaced.
- **City Agriculture & City Veterinary Emails:** `/contact-us/` (`mlyblyagri2023@gmail.com`, `citymalaybalay@gmail.com`) vs `/departments-offices/` (`mlyblyagri@yahoo.com`, `cityvetmalaybalay@gmail.com`). Both variants are listed.
- **CSP Local Logo Mirroring:** Because `vercel.json` strict `img-src` Content Security Policy blocks hotlinking arbitrary external image hosts (`sakata.malaybalaycity.gov.ph`), all 28 verified official emblems were downloaded, aspect-ratio preserved (max 384px), and stored under `public/images/offices/` with full primary source attribution.
